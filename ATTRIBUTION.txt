EventOwner uses original procedural pixels and waveforms generated by Saurav K. Aryal. New code and these procedural samples are distributed under the repository MIT license.

STARSS23 upstream reference: Kazuki Shimada et al. 2023. STARSS23: An Audio-Visual Dataset of Spatial Recordings of Real Scenes with Spatiotemporal Annotations of Sound Events. NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks. https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.09126
Official data source: https://zenodo.org/records/7880637
The upstream notice is retained at work/task09_design_sources/LICENSE. Recording identifiers and checksums, saved numerical features/results and derived statistics are provided for reproducibility. Raw real audio/video and participant photographs are not redistributed. Optional replay must obtain the exact source files independently and observe their upstream terms.

The procedural generator is phenomenological and includes simple active-intensity spatial rendering. No third-party pretrained audio/video generator weights or generated derivatives are included. Future comparison models and recordings retain their individual terms; current PhysWave checkpoints and generated audio have noncommercial restrictions.

Historical PoseSplit attribution is preserved in archive/posesplit_20261031/ATTRIBUTION.txt.

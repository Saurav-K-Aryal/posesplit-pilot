EventOwner An Exact Marginal Pilot of Audio Visual Source Ownership
Saurav K. Aryal, Howard University
November 1, 2026

A controlled shared-state renderer creates actual RGB and FOA media for two sources. A 2-by-2 ownership crossing preserves exact audio/video marginals across labels. Frozen 120-scene confirmation produces 480 dependent pairs. Frequency-resolved joint balanced accuracy is 0.80625 [scene 95% interval 0.764583, 0.847917], broadband joint 0.770833 [0.725, 0.816667], temporal 0.5 and pooled spatial 0.510417. The paired difference interval is [0.00625, 0.064583], but both joint ranking accuracies are 0.995833. Near-colocation and overlap remain difficult. Real 72-window matched/shuffled association is inconclusive and a simpler reference is stronger. The result supports controlled joint construction/evaluation feasibility; learned acoustics and real transfer remain untested. See README.md, paper.pdf, overleaf.zip and verify_saved_results.py. Historical PoseSplit is archived separately.
